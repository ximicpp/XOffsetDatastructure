# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone who
participates in XOffsetDatastructure, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Expected Behavior

- Communicate constructively, politely, and in good faith during code reviews, issue discussions, and
  benchmark debates.
- Focus feedback on technical facts, reproducible evidence, and user impact rather than individuals.
- Respect differing viewpoints and accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Help newcomers navigate the codebase, build requirements, and contribution guidelines.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, insults, or derogatory remarks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or bad-faith spamming of issues or pull requests.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional technical
  community.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Maintainers may edit,
remove, or reject comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that violate this Code of Conduct, and
may restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.

To report a Code of Conduct concern, please contact the repository maintainers directly via GitHub.
